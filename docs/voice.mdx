# Voice

Neore Chat can listen and speak. In **hands-free voice mode** you talk,
your words are sent as a message, and the reply is read aloud — then
it listens again, so you can hold a spoken conversation without
touching the keyboard.

## Hands-free voice mode

**Starting and stopping:**

1. Click the hands-free voice button in the composer
   (tooltip: **Hands-free voice mode — talk, and replies are read
   aloud**)
2. Allow microphone access if your browser asks
3. Speak. What you say appears in the composer as you talk, and is
   sent when you pause
4. The reply is read aloud, then the mode listens for your next
   message
5. Press **Esc** or click the button again to stop

The button shows what the mode is doing: **Listening…**,
**Thinking…** while the reply is written, and **Speaking…** while it
is read aloud. If nothing is said for a while, the mode stops by
itself with **No speech detected. Hands-free mode stopped.**

### Which voice reads the reply

For signed-in users, replies are spoken with a hosted voice
(MiniMax Speech). The audio is made for playback only — it is not
saved to the chat, and no audio message is added to the thread.

You fall back to your **browser's built-in voice** when:

- you are using a guest account — create an account to use the
  hosted voice
- you have used up today's spoken-reply allowance (see below)
- the hosted voice fails for any reason — the rest of the reply
  continues in the browser voice, so it is never cut off or spoken
  twice

### Choosing a voice

Pick the hosted voice at **Settings → App → Customization**, in the
**Chat** section, under **Hands-free voice**. The setting is not
shown to guest accounts.

A [skill](/docs/skills) can set its own voice. When a reply comes
from that skill — you invoked it with `/skill-name`, or it answered
in a group chat — the skill's voice is used instead of yours. A skill
voice can be one of the hosted voices or a voice installed in your
browser.

## Daily allowance and cost

Spoken replies count against a **daily character allowance**:

| Plan    | Characters per day |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| Free    | 10,000             |
| Premium | 200,000            |

Once it is used up, replies are read with your browser's voice until
the next day.

The hosted voice is also charged to your credits by the number of
characters spoken, like any other model call. If you add your own
**fal.ai** key under **Settings → API Keys**, speech runs on your key
instead and is billed by fal.ai.

## Other ways to hear replies

These always use your **browser's voice**, never the hosted one, and
cost nothing:

- **Read aloud** — the speaker button in a reply's action bar reads
  that one reply. Click **Stop reading** to stop.
- **Auto-read replies** — at **Settings → App → Customization**, in
  the **Chat** section, reads every reply aloud when it finishes.

To create an audio file of a reply that is saved in the chat, use
**Generate voice** in the reply's action bar instead.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** "Microphone access denied"
- **Solution:** Allow microphone access for Neore in your browser's
  site settings, then start hands-free mode again.

**Issue:** "Speech recognition is not supported in your browser"
- **Solution:** Your browser has no built-in speech recognition, and
  Neore's hosted transcription was not available. Sign in with a full
  account, or try a browser with speech recognition, such as a
  current Chrome or Edge.

**Issue:** The reply is read in a robotic voice
- **Solution:** You are hearing the browser voice. Check whether you
  are signed in with a full account and whether you used up today's
  allowance.
